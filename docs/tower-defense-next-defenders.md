# Two complementary defender designs

Status: generated concept art and proposed mechanics only. Neither is recruitable in the current seven-defender roster. Complete the fifty-wave release before starting their gameplay milestone. All numbers below are provisional design assumptions.

## Pepe Ballista

Role: deliberate long-range anti-armor shots, distinct from the rapid-fire Archer and area-damage Relic Cannon. Visual: Pepe crew, mechanical crossbow/windlass, enormous emerald bolt and matching jade/gold turret.

Proposed base: 185 defense shards, 95 damage, 300 range, 1.6s base interval. Ignore half the target's remaining armor after acid, without permanently changing armor. One target at base. Three planned paths: Spearhead (bolt damage and eventual line penetration), Siege Windlass (reload speed and boss pressure), Watchtower (range and targeting reach). Each would retain 5/2/0 limits and shared move/sell.

Prototype gate: visible bolt impact, line collision with a clear target cap, consistent armor/acid calculation and a mixed-defense test against shield walls. No income effects or base healing.

## Pepe Thornkeeper

Role: road traps that punish groups and provide another way to handle fast specters without relying entirely on slows. Visual: recognizable Pepe, leaf cloak, living-wood staff, thorn pods and a jade/gold planter turret.

Proposed base: 155 shards; place a short-lived thorn patch on the nearest path segment within 140 range every 2s. Each patch holds ten 8-damage charges, expires after 8s, and a tower may maintain at most three patches. Charges apply once per enemy crossing; armor still works normally. Three planned paths: Briar Thorns (damage), Seedbank (charges and patch persistence), Wild Reach (placement range and coverage).

Prototype gate: visible path placement, deterministic crossing/charge consumption, no repeated per-frame hits, bounded patch counts, and clean pause/move/sell/replay behavior. Boss damage needs a separate balance pass. Trap damage is not slow immunity bypass or unbounded damage-over-time stacking.
