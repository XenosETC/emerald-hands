      How to play

      Move diagonally on dark squares. Jumps are mandatory. If a piece can jump again, keep going with that piece. Reach the far edge to become a king and move both ways. Crowning ends the turn.

      Last move squares are outlined. Capture destinations have a ring.

      Use arrow keys to move between playable squares and Enter or Space to select. Press Escape to clear a selection.
