# Pepe Poker reactions

The poker table gets a small set of illustrated Pepe reactions. Each one should read at seat-badge size and feel like it came from the arcade.

| Reaction | Art direction | Table moment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rocket Meltdown | Panicked Pepe riding an emerald rocket through a big cartoon explosion, inspired by the arcade rocket games | A bluff or a big hand goes up in flames |
| Berserk Pepe | Our angry Berserk Pepe pet with clenched fists, red eyes, and a flare of rage | A brutal river or a bad beat |
| Royal Smug | Crowned Pepe grinning beside a gold poker chip | A bluff gets through |
| Busted Pepe | Pepe in tears clutching his last chip | The stack goes to zero |

Future ideas include a suspicious side-eye for a big raise, a sleeping Pepe for the tank, and short animated versions of Rocket Meltdown and Berserk Pepe with static fallbacks.

Reactions appear briefly at the sender’s seat. Players choose when to send them. Keep them visually clear of the cards and controls, and out of hand history.
