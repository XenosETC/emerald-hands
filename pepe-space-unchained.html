Horizontal shooter

Fly the chain-space frontier. Clear corrupt ships and survive each vault-node wave.

Controls

Touch and drag to fly, move the mouse, or use WASD / arrows. Space, a mouse click, or the Burst button spends 5 shards for a burst; your main gun fires automatically. E or Repair spends 10 flight shards for up to 25 shield, with an 8-second cooldown.

Keep moving

Dashed aim lines warn of a shot for the last 0.28 seconds before firing. Dodge the red, white-rimmed shots. Bosses also fire two spread shots. A wide screen gives more room to read them.
