Full scheduled roster and starting stats for this wave. Armor reduces damage; slow resistance reduces slow strength. Combat damage and acid can change live values.

    Wave intelligence

    Jade Tyrant - wave 35

    25% armor, 25% slow resistance; breach: 6. Two shieldbearers and two specters follow at 1.1s intervals. Ballista and acid counter his armor.

    Relic Sovereign - wave 50

    Final boss: 30% armor, 40% slow resistance; breach: 10. Two shieldbearers, one brute and three specters follow at 1.1s intervals. Use Strong for boss damage and First for leaks. Clear the whole wave to win.

    Shieldbearer - wave 21+

    40% armor; breach: 3 relic health. Alchemist acid and concentrated fire break the shield wall.

    Emerald specter - wave 26+

    Fast; reduces the strength of slows by 60%. Rapid lasers and long-range damage stop it. Breach: 1 relic health.

    Goblin raider - wave 3+

    Fast, low health. Rapid-fire towers and slowing fields stop a rush. Breach: 1 relic health.

    Goblin brute - wave 7+

    20% damage resistance, slower movement. Slow and focus fire. Breach: 2. The wave 20 warboss resists 25% and deals 5 breach damage; two brutes and two raiders follow at 1.1s intervals.

    50 waves. Bosses every 5 waves; major bosses on 20, 35 and 50, with fixed guard formations on all three major waves. Shieldbearers from wave 21; slow-resistant specters from wave 26. Health and formation density rise gradually; enemy speed growth caps at wave 15. Each cleared wave gives a build break.

    How to play
    Build & defend

    Protect the vault. Place defenders on glowing pads, then send the next wave.

    Controls

    Select a defender and tap a pad, or drag a tower onto it. Select a placed tower to choose its upgrade path, move it between waves, or sell for a 70% refund. Upgrade up to two paths: one to tier 5 and a secondary to tier 2. The third path locks. Keys 1-9 select defenders. Sunwarden is limited to two active heroes; press H or tap Emerald Nova during a wave.

    Plan the next wave

    Mix damage and slowing towers. The highlighted card is your selected defender; each purchase uses in-game shards.
