One-tap runner

Read the road. Time the jump. Collect emeralds and avoid purple spikes. Gold frog statues turn the world cosmic for 8 seconds: run 35% faster and smash spikes. A gold ring shows your shield through a brief protected slowdown. An exit pulse clears nearby spikes once as protection ends; jump the purple shards beyond it.

Controls

Tap, Click, or Space or the Jump button to jump. A tap just before landing is remembered briefly. Crowns grant six seconds of Royal Skyride: soar through optional style gates and land protected. Emerald frog statues grant seven seconds of Emerald Rush: green eyes attract emeralds from farther away, but spikes remain dangerous. Powers have independent timers; only Cosmic Disco adds speed. Blue storm auras do not change spike collisions. Landscape gives more room to read the road.
